# Imágenes de Luma

Recursos creados con la herramienta integrada ImageGen. Las imágenes WebP se encuentran dentro del proyecto. Las instrucciones de producción conservan el vocabulario en inglés que se utilizó para generarlas.

**Océano, `public/assets/ocean.webp`.** Editorial marine science hero photograph for an elegant science learning dashboard. Ultra-wide horizontal panoramic 3:1 underwater composition. A big stunning translucent luminous orange-coral peach moon jellyfish with detailed radial bell and long delicate fuchsia peach filaments, swimming diagonally in the right half. Two smaller lavender and coral luminescent jellyfish on the far right in the distance. Rich deep indigo navy water with tiny delicate plankton, dark empty open water on the left 45 percent for readable text to be added separately. Medium format macro ocean photography, dreamy cinematic light. Edge-to-edge photograph, no words or graphics.

**Lumi, `public/assets/lumi.webp` y `public/assets/lumi-cutout.webp`.** A happy little lavender axolotl scientist, in the style of a premium art vinyl figurine, seated front view, waving a tiny lavender hand. Large rounded lavender head with three external feathery plum and pink gills on each side, two glossy onyx eyes behind thin perfectly round violet eyeglass frames, small friendly smile, pale lilac belly. Wearing a little ivory lab coat, tiny dark violet notebook held against body. Whole body, feet and tail visible. Center on a very pale lavender background with a soft contact shadow. Refined tactile matte polymer, diffused studio lighting. No words. For the cutout, preserve the full character, pose, face, glasses and book. Remove the floor and background and retain transparent pixels on every side.

**Astronomía, `public/assets/nebula.webp`.** Editorial photo of a spectacular stellar nursery, Carina-nebula-inspired cosmic dust pillars, warm amber ochre illuminated edges on red-brown clouds in the lower right contrasted with deep petrol teal night sky, finely scattered stars, expansive empty dark left third. Authentic astrophotography aesthetic. Cinematic wide 3:1 banner photo composition for a science magazine, subtle depth, no diagrams, text, logos or labels.

**Botánica, `public/assets/plant.webp`.** Photorealistic premium nature journal panoramic 3:1 photograph, luminous green leaves showing translucent vein structures bathed in golden sunlight in the right half, dark deep forest green shallow depth of field background on the left half. High-end macro camera, textured leaf networks and subtle moss green, soft shadows, natural and scientific with no diagrams, type or decorative overlay.
